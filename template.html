How this works. Predictions come from a gradient-boosted model (XGBoost) trained on 1999–2025 NFL games — Elo ratings, rolling offensive/defensive EPA-per-play efficiency, QB-specific Elo, QB-continuity signals, rest days, and matchup context. Backtested accuracy above is on held-out seasons the model never trained on. Early-season weeks lean more on the prior season's data since there's little current-season form yet. This is a snapshot generated at build time, not a live feed — ask for a refresh as the season progresses. For fun and analysis, not betting advice.
